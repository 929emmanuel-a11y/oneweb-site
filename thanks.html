Common questions

How do you know which website is mine?

Every website we build has its own site code (like KPOST-378B). The payment link we sent you carries that code, so your payment is matched to your site no matter which phone number or email you used. You'll see your business name and site code at the top of this page and in your welcome email. Log in to the portal with the email you paid with and your site will be there.

I paid from a different phone number or email. Is that a problem?

No. Your site is matched by its site code, not by your phone number. If you ever log in with a different email or text us from a new number, just send us your site code and we'll connect it.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your account was created when you paid. Just log in with your email and the code we send you.

When will I be charged for hosting?

If you chose hosting, the $39/month starts 30 days after today. You can update your card, see receipts, or cancel anytime from the Billing tab in your portal.

What if I change my mind?

If your site hasn't been published yet, you can get a full refund within 7 days. See our refund policy.

I didn't get the welcome email.

Give it a few minutes and check spam. Still nothing? Email hello@onewebonline.com with your business name and we'll get you in.
