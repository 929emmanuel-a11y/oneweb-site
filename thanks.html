Thanks for trusting us with your website. Your site is already saved to your account. Here's what happens next.

  1. Check your email

    In the next few minutes you'll get a welcome email from One Web, sent to the email you used to pay. It has your login link. Not there? Check your spam or promotions folder.

  2. Log in to your client portal

    Go to onewebonline.com/portal and enter that same email. We'll send you a 6-digit code: no password, nothing to sign up for. Your website is already in there.

  3. Make it yours

    Add your photos and logo, update your services and hours, and list the towns you serve. Want something changed? Just describe it in plain words and we'll handle it.

    Already own a domain like yourbusiness.com? Connect it in the Domain tab. Don't have one? We'll help you pick one. When it looks right, hit Publish and you're live.

    We'll check in to help with your domain and any edits, so you're not doing this alone.

Common questions

How do you know which website is mine?

Your payment is tied to the phone number we contacted you on, so the site we built for your business is linked to your account automatically. Log in with the email you paid with and it'll be there.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your account was created when you paid. Just log in with your email and the code we send you.

When will I be charged for hosting?

If you chose hosting, the $39/month starts 30 days after today. You can update your card, see receipts, or cancel anytime from the Billing tab in your portal.

What if I change my mind?

If your site hasn't been published yet, you can get a full refund within 7 days. See our refund policy.

I didn't get the welcome email.

Give it a few minutes and check spam. Still nothing? Email hello@onewebonline.com with your business name and we'll get you in.
