Refund Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy covers purchases from One Web, a brand of Dolyn ("we", "us").

Free previews

Website previews are free. You only pay if you decide to buy.

Website design fee (one-time)

If you change your mind within 7 days of paying and your site has not been published yet, we will refund the full amount. Once your site has been published, the design fee is not refundable, but we will keep fixing anything that does not match what we agreed to in writing.

Hosting (monthly)

Hosting is billed month to month and you can cancel anytime by texting or emailing us. Cancelling stops future charges. We do not refund partial months. If you cancel, we will send you your site files on request.

Problems on our side

If your site is down for more than 24 hours in a row because of a problem on our end, tell us and we will credit that month of hosting.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@onewebonline.com or text (214) 743-5303 with your business name. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
