Websites for Hair Salons That Bring In Calls

A strong online presence is essential for hair salons to attract new clients and keep chairs full. One Web creates custom websites built to turn visitors into bookings.

Why Hair Salons Need Better Websites

Most people search for hair salons on their phones, often wanting to book quickly or see recent work. Over 70% of salon searches happen on mobile devices, and many clients use Google Maps to find and choose a nearby salon. Positive reviews, up-to-date photos, and simple booking options make the difference between a new client and a lost opportunity.

What Customers Look For in a Hair Salon Website

  • Mobile-first experience: If your site isn’t fast and easy on a phone, you’ll lose bookings.
  • Simple booking: Clients want to book an appointment instantly, not wait for a call back.
  • Real photos: High-quality images of your actual work inspire trust and bookings.
  • Reviews front and center: Most clients check reviews before choosing a salon.
  • Clear service details: List what you offer and prices, so there’s no confusion.
  • Easy contact: Click-to-call, quote, or booking forms help you get more clients with less effort.

Common Hair Salon Website Mistakes

Many salons struggle with outdated visuals, cluttered designs, or missing information. Common issues include:

  • Slow site speed that drives visitors away.
  • Old or generic photos that don’t reflect your work.
  • No clear way to request an appointment.
  • Hidden or outdated contact information.
  • Few or no reviews on display.
  • Lack of local search optimization, so your salon doesn’t show up where it should.
