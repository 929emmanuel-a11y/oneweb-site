Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

One Web is a brand of Dolyn ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Public business information from public business listings (business name, business phone number, address, website, hours, category, ratings, reviews and photos), used to contact businesses and build website previews.
  • Information you give us when you text, call or email us: your name, phone number, email, and your messages.
  • Basic website data such as pages visited, collected by standard server logs.

How we use it

  • To respond to you and send the website preview you asked for.
  • To build, launch and support your website if you become a customer.
  • To process payments (handled by our payment processor; we do not store card numbers).

Text messaging (SMS)

We only send text messages to people who text us first or ask us to contact them. Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, and HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent or share your phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our telecom provider).

Phone calls

We may call business phone numbers listed publicly. If you'd rather not hear from us, say so on a call, reply STOP to a text, or email us, and we will add your number to our do-not-contact list.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business (hosting, telecom, payment processing, AI tools that help write website content), and only as needed for those services, or when required by law.

Retention

Website previews are removed if you don't move forward. We keep customer records as long as needed to provide service and meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing hello@onewebonline.com.
