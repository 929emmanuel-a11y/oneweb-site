Questions

Good to know.

Don't see your question here? Text or email us. A real reply, not a ticket number.

How long does a project take?

Most projects go from kickoff to launch in two to three weeks, depending on how many pages you need and how quickly feedback comes back. Larger sites take longer, and we'll give you a timeline up front.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted individually based on scope. Once we understand what you need, you'll get a clear, fixed price in writing before any work begins, with no surprise invoices.

Do I need to have a domain already?

No. If you already have one, we'll launch on it. If not, we'll help you choose and set one up in your name.

What do you need from me?

Very little. We start from what's already public about your business, then ask you to confirm details, share any photos or logos you'd like used, and approve the design.

Can I make changes after launch?

Yes. Every launch includes a round of edits, and ongoing updates are available whenever your business changes.
